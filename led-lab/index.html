Experimental readings

All measurements are editable. Enter voltage in V and current in mA.

Measurement safety: Never exceed 30 mA. Measure current with the ammeter in series and voltage directly across the LED. Keep the power supply OFF when changing connections. The original procedure specifies ten readings starting at 0.60 V (red) and 0.80 V (green), increasing by 0.050 V. If no rising-current region is captured, knee voltage cannot be estimated from those points.

How to use this app

  1. Enter the measured voltage and current for each LED. Use + Add reading if you have more than ten points.
  2. Press Auto-select fit points to suggest the rising region, or tick the Fit boxes manually for a portion that looks approximately straight on the graph.
  3. Open I–V graph to inspect both curves and their straight-line extrapolations.
  4. Open Calculations to see knee voltage, Planck's constant, mean value and percentage error.
  5. Use Export CSV or Print / Save PDF to retain the experimental results.

The knee voltage is estimated using the voltage-axis intercept of a fitted straight line. This is an approximate experimental method, not a precisely defined LED turn-on point.
