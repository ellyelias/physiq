How to use PhysiQ for our study

Follow the steps below that match your situation.

If you're one of the research study students (pretest/posttest)

The order below is IMPORTANT — don't skip any step, since your teacher's research results depend on it.

1

Pretest — once, at the very start

Open the Research · Pretest/Posttest tab first — before Student Practice or Notes. Enter your full name (same spelling every time) and your group, then complete the Pretest.

2

Practice — throughout the study period

Use the Notes tab to review each topic, and Student Practice to answer questions. Do this as often as your teacher asks, for the next few weeks.

3

Posttest — once, when your teacher says the study has ended

Go back to Research · Pretest/Posttest and complete the Posttest. Use the exact same name and group as your Pretest.

Each test (Pretest/Posttest) can only be taken ONCE under the name you register. The app blocks a second attempt automatically — so don't rush, and make sure your name is typed correctly.

If you're just using PhysiQ for regular practice

There's no special order — go straight to the Notes tab to read topic summaries, or straight to Student Practice to start practising questions on the topics of your choice.
